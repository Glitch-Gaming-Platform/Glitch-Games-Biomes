01  —  Exotic Matter Refinery
Ashline Containment Works
Contamination Sort & Certify
Route samples to the correct chamber before contamination spreads.

Core Mechanic Concept
The player operates a four-lane sample intake conveyor. Each incoming canister displays an instability reading (green / yellow / red glow). The player must route stable canisters to the matching processing chamber (fuel cell, filter press, or stabilization tank) and quarantine unstable canisters before they contaminate adjacent lanes. Once a batch is routed, the correct certified output is matched to the waiting customer's ticket.

Unique Twist
A contamination meter fills if an unstable canister is routed to a live chamber. If the meter maxes, a hazard alarm triggers: all current canisters are lost, a cleanup penalty is assessed, and the queue is paused for a cooldown. Higher purity grades pay more but run the contamination risk higher — the player must decide when to chase premium output.

	•	The quarantine bay can be voluntarily cleared mid-shift using a "decon flush" action, but this consumes a spent filter from stock and pauses intake for 3 seconds.

02  —  Biome Maintenance & Repair Company
North Anchor Repair Shed
Fault Diagnosis Tree
Read the symptom. Branch through the diagnostic tree. Commit to the right repair before the Biome fails.

Core Mechanic Concept
Customers describe a Biome fault using symptom language ("flickering horizon," "gravity skipping," "rooms drifting forward in time"). The player sees a branching diagnostic panel — a visual decision tree with two to three branch points. Each branch asks the player to categorise the fault further (structural vs. environmental, minor vs. critical, etc.) before the three offer buttons unlock. Only the correctly diagnosed branch reveals the right offer; choosing without completing the tree reveals all three offers but at reduced accuracy.

Unique Twist
The patience timer and reward are tied to severity, not just time. A timeline leak customer has an extremely short patience window (the leak gets worse each second) but pays the highest reward. A standard inspection customer has a long patience window but pays the least. If the player shortcuts diagnosis on a leak-level fault, a wrong offer consumes parts and does nothing — the customer escalates to an emergency state, penalising reputation far more than a timeout would.

Inspect Anchor, Tune Climate, Patch Timeline Leak — locked until at least one diagnostic branch is selected.

	•	A customer in "emergency escalation" state blocks the queue — no new customers enter until they are either resolved or dismissed at a satisfaction penalty.
	•	Inspector visits store a record of the last three offer choices. If two of three are wrong branch choices, the shop receives a compliance warning that reduces the queue's max patience timers for the next shift.

03  —  Biome Design Studio
Glassyard Biome Studio
Mood Palette Assembly
Decode the client's aesthetic intent. Assemble the matching palette. Beat the trend window.

Core Mechanic Concept
Customers describe their desired habitat in mood language rather than direct product names ("something that feels like a storm at sea," "ancient and alive," "minimal, almost cold"). The player sees a palette board with nine style chips arranged in a 3x3 grid — three terrain chips, three lighting chips, three decor chips. Each chip carries hidden style tags. The player selects one chip from each category to assemble a three-chip palette that matches the customer's stated mood. A match score is calculated based on tag overlap. A "trend meter" in the corner shows which style tags are currently popular; matching trending tags adds a bonus satisfaction multiplier.

Unique Twist
Luxury customers require a perfect-tag match (three-of-three) and have very low patience. Budget customers accept a two-of-three match. The trend meter shifts at the start of each new shift — chips that were trending last shift may now be neutral or even penalised. Players who memorise trends earn a pricing bonus on top of match score.

3x3 chip palette board
Nine chips: terrain row, lighting row, decor row. Each chip shows its visual but hides its style tags until selected.
Assembled palette preview
Shows the three selected chips side by side with a running match score and tag reveal.
Trend meter sidebar
Small bar chart showing current top three trending style tags; updates at shift start.
Client tier indicator
Budget / Standard / Luxury icon on the ticket, controlling match threshold and patience timer.

Regional

Edge Cases
	•	If the player swaps a chip after committing the palette, the match score recalculates but a "second-guess" penalty is applied to the satisfaction score regardless of whether the swap improved the match.
	•	A chip can go out of stock mid-shift (rare material shortage event); the player must substitute from remaining chips, which may drop the match score below threshold for a luxury client.
	•	If a luxury client's match score is exactly two of three, they leave a public review that suppresses the next two clients' patience timers by 20%.

04  —  Security & Defense Contractor
Redoubt Contract Yard
Threat Assessment & Readiness Board
Classify the threat level. Check squad readiness. Deploy the right coverage before the client panics.

Core Mechanic Concept
Customers arrive with an intel card describing a security problem. The card shows multiple signals: location type, threat descriptor words, duration, and a "client composure" indicator (calm / anxious / panicking). The player must read these signals and classify the threat into one of three tiers (low / medium / high) before selecting the correct response offer. A squad readiness board shows how many guards are currently deployed elsewhere — if all guards are out, urgent responses require pulling one back (adding delay and a pulled-contract fee). After classifying, the player selects the matching service and confirms gear availability.

Unique Twist
Misclassifying a high-threat client as low-threat causes a mission failure event in the field — the guard is marked injured, the client requests a refund, and reputation drops significantly. A "panic timer" overlays on panicking clients: if not served before it hits zero, the client contacts a competitor and the yard loses the contract for the week.

05  —  Portal Transit Company
Eastgate Portal Office
Route Traffic Controller
Assign each arrival to the right gate. Watch the fuel. Flag unstable links before someone gets lost in transit.

Core Mechanic Concept
The player manages a gate board showing four to eight portal gates, each linked to a specific destination. Passengers and cargo requests arrive on a boarding display at the top of the screen with a destination tag, cargo type flag (passenger / standard cargo / fragile / hazmat), and a safety flag if the route's stability is degraded. The player must drag or assign each arrival to the correct gate, verify the gate has sufficient fuel, and flag any assignment to a degraded route for a safety hold. Each gate can queue one to three assignments; exceeding that causes a congestion event.

Unique Twist
Route stability degrades passively in real time. A gate whose route is at yellow stability still functions but applies a late-delivery penalty to any cargo. A gate at red stability cannot be used — assignments to it are auto-rejected. The player can spend a fuel cell from stock to temporarily stabilise a red route, but this takes three seconds and the queue keeps arriving. Passenger jumps and cargo slots compete for the same gate — a large cargo booking locks a gate for a longer window, which can strand passengers if not managed.

Fuel reserve panel
Shows total fuel cells in stock; each stabilisation action consumes one.

06  —  Biome Farming & Rare Foods
Southplot Rare Foods
Freshness Triage Counter
Match freshness grade to the right buyer. Clear aging stock wisely. Never let the clinic get wilted herbs.

Core Mechanic Concept
Harvested stock arrives from the field into tiered bins on the left side of the counter — bins labelled Fresh, Good, Aging, and Near-Spoil, each with a visible spoilage timer. Customer tickets specify both produce type (crop bundle / medicinal herbs / tasting box) and a minimum freshness grade. The player must pull stock from the correct bin, match the produce type, and serve it before the produce in that bin spoils. A "discount decision" mechanic applies to Aging and Near-Spoil bins: the player can mark them at a reduced price to attract budget customers, or hold them for standard customers at the risk of spoilage.

Unique Twist
Selling Aging-grade medicinal herbs to a clinic-tier customer causes a quality complaint and a satisfaction penalty — clinics require Fresh or Good only, indicated by a red freshness requirement badge on their ticket. The player must read the badge and refuse if stock doesn't meet the grade, which lowers the ticket count but protects satisfaction. Rare climate produce (snow berries, sky fruits) only appears in limited stock and commands a multiplied reward — the player must decide whether to sell it immediately or hold it for a premium collector.

07  —  Weapons & Tools
Cinderlane Tool Forge
Gear-Type & Material Matching
Identify the gear. Match the material. Use the right process — or the output is substandard and the customer comes back angry.

Core Mechanic Concept
Customers arrive with a piece of gear (visually shown on the ticket as a worn weapon, tool, or scanner icon) and a service need. The gear type determines which service applies (weapons take a tune, tools take a repair, scanners take a calibration). The player must select the correct service and then choose the correct material from the forge stock to fulfil it. Higher-tier gear requires rarer material — using a common material on a rare-tier weapon produces a substandard result that fails the ticket. The forge stock panel shows current material quantities and rarity levels.

Unique Twist
Gear has a durability history visible on the ticket. A piece of gear that has been repaired multiple times shows increased wear — the third repair of the same item requires an upgrade material (not just standard), or the repair outputs at degraded quality. This creates a "return customer" economy: recognising a repeat customer's gear and selecting the upgrade path earns a loyalty bonus, while applying the standard path earns a complaint.

08  —  Magic Goods
Moonstall Ward Shop
Risk-Outcome Matcher
Read the threat. Match the remedy. Handle unstable stock before it expires — or worse, misfires.

Core Mechanic Concept
Customers describe a problem using outcome language rather than product names ("I need to stop something from following me," "my walls keep bleeding through," "I feel sick from the air in my Biome"). The player must assess the risk level implied by the description and match it to the correct remedy tier: charm (low risk), potion (medium risk), or ward (high risk). The stock panel shows each remedy's quantity, expiry timer, and instability rating. Unstable potions and wards carry a misfire risk — the player can choose to sell them at a discount or dispose of them safely.

Unique Twist
Each item in stock has an expiry timer running in real time. Potions that expire mid-shift become "anomalous" — they cannot be sold, must be safely disposed of using a containment action, and if ignored they trigger a shop anomaly event that penalises satisfaction for multiple customers. Higher-instability items pay more when sold but raise the misfire probability. The player must balance selling unstable stock quickly against the risk of a misfire complaint.

	•	If an anomalous item is not disposed of within its zero-expiry window, it triggers a shop anomaly event: a visible cloud effect in the UI, a 10-second queue pause, and a satisfaction deduction applied to all customers currently waiting.

09  —  Exploration Guide
Westtrail Guide Table
Route Freshness & Safety Assessment
Check the map's freshness. Match the client's ambition to a route you can actually vouch for.

Core Mechanic Concept
Customers state a destination and purpose (sightseeing, resource gathering, or dangerous-site survey). The player checks the route card on a map display — each route shows a freshness rating (current / recent / stale / expired) and a danger tier (safe / moderate / hazardous). The player must match the destination to the correct service tier (route briefing for safe/moderate routes, guided expedition for any tier with escort, danger read for hazardous-only). If the map is stale or expired for the requested route, the player must flag it rather than serve it — sending a client on expired map data causes a field injury event.

Unique Twist
Map freshness decays in real time. A route that was "current" at the start of the shift may be "recent" by midway through. The player can trigger a "map refresh" action on a specific route, but it takes a few seconds and costs a supply token. Nervous clients (shown by a fidgeting animation) are more likely to injure themselves on moderate routes — the player can choose to upsell them to a guided expedition instead of a briefing, which pays more but consumes a guide slot from the team roster.

Service buttons
Route Briefing, Guided Expedition, Danger Read — all visible; Danger Read only valid on hazardous routes.

Button per route card; triggers a brief animation consuming a supply token and restoring freshness to "current."

Customer Types

The player must match the cargo type to the correct service tier and confirm the route before dispatch. Time-sensitive and medicine cargo have independent countdown timers that run from acceptance to dispatch.

Unique Twist
Failed deliveries (wrong service tier, route hazard not flagged, medicine timer expired) reduce the trust rating by one rung. Building the trust ladder requires a streak of successful deliveries in each tier before the next tier unlocks. A higher trust rating not only unlocks cargo types but also raises the base pay on all tiers. This means the early game is low-value but safe, while the late game is high-value but a single mistake has more consequences.

19  —  Hospitality / Inn / Hotel / Shelter
Lanternrest Road Inn
Dual-Mode Front Desk with Room QA
Book the right room type. Run the QA check after each checkout. Flip to crisis mode when the town needs shelter.

Core Mechanic Concept
Guests arrive at the front desk requesting lodging. Three room types are available: standard room (travelers and tourists), shelter bed (displaced or budget guests), and room-meal bundle (guests who want both lodging and dining). The player must match the guest's stated need to the correct room type, check room availability on the room board, and confirm that the assigned room has passed its last QA check (cleanliness status: clean / needs check / dirty). After each checkout, a room enters "needs check" status and must be QA-cleared before the next booking — rushing a dirty room to a premium guest triggers a review penalty.

Unique Twist
Crisis mode: during a Biome failure or town disaster event, a crisis banner activates. In crisis mode, shelter bed requests flood the queue at double the normal rate, patience timers are shortened (displaced guests are desperate), and prices are reduced (civic rate). Successfully clearing crisis waves earns civic reputation points that act as a discount on permit renewals and contribute to a "civic trust" bonus. Players who have over-booked their standard rooms before a crisis hits will find no shelter beds available — a choice that reflects badly on the inn's civic standing.

Global Rules Reminder
All 19 mini-games inherit the following shared rules from the customer simulator framework:

Rule
Detail
Queue mechanics
Customers queue in order. Patience is a countdown timer. Short patience = high urgency customer type.
Offer matching
The player must select the exact matching offer (and have required stock) to win the ticket.
Wrong offer
Fails the ticket, resets streak, lowers satisfaction and reputation.
Patience expiry
Customer leaves with same penalty as a wrong offer.
Reward formula
max(ticket reward, offer reward) + first-daily bonus.
Streak rewards
Collectible every 5-streak, decor unlock after 20 served, badge after 50 served.
Growth scaling
Higher tier increases queue size, stock pressure, patience pressure, and branch/empire dependencies.

Each business mini-game layers a unique mechanic on top of this framework. The global rules ensure all businesses feel familiar to approach; the unique mechanic ensures each one feels different to master.

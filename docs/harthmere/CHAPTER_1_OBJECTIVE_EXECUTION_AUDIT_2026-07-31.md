# Chapter 1 objective-by-objective execution audit

Date: 2026-07-31

Status: planning and repair in progress. Production deployment is explicitly out of scope.

## Release-gate contract for every objective

Each objective must pass all three layers before Chapter 1 is considered complete:

1. **Unit/contract:** authored instruction, exact target, interaction owner, item source/grant/consume chain, dialogue exit guidance, and successor tracking are deterministic.
2. **Automated E2E:** a fresh actor completes the step through the actual product interaction without fixtures that replace Chapter-authored pickups or supplies.
3. **Live browser:** the target is visible, the instruction is readable and unambiguous, the interaction works, inventory/progress visibly changes, and the next objective is tracked in the journal, HUD, map, and minimap.

## Known failure from the 2026-07-31 21:47 production HAR

`Gather Parts` routed the player to Luis's Repair Cart and accepted repeated completion requests, but required 4 scrap metal, 2 iron ingots, and 1 tree resin that the marked location did not visibly provide. The browser E2E fixture seeded those external materials and therefore masked the broken player experience. That fixture behavior is not acceptable for a linear Chapter 1 objective.

## Material-acquisition contract

Chapter 1 now reuses the existing Gimme Shelter/Workbench acquisition guide instead of inventing a second quest-only supply system.

- Every ordinary material requirement appears in the selected quest with its exact quantity.
- Each available **gather**, **buy**, and **craft** route is presented as a player choice.
- Every location-backed route has a **Show on map** action that writes the normal active destination used by the world map, minimap, and HUD.
- Story-owned plot items (breakfast tea, AUGUR-9's core cell, Compound B, and Sorrel's ledger) are never presented as commodities; their prior Chapter 1 grant is the source.
- Provisioning categories resolve to real accepted inventory items: clean water, road rations, hearty stew, herb bundles, wall lanterns, road repair kits, field medkits, coal, a warm cloak, rope, and iron ingots.
- Luis's Repair Cart remains the hand-in point for Gather Parts and is rendered as a visible cart while that objective is active. The cart is not a magic supply crate; the player chooses how to obtain the materials first.

The static material gate fails if any Chapter 1 commodity has no real acquisition route or no map-trackable option.
